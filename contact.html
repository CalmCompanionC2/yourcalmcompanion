Norman is a weighted Highland cow plush with gentle haptic vibration inside. He is a calm, screen-free companion that you hold. He is not a toy and not a medical device.

Glass bead fill, for deep pressure comfort. The weight is part of the experience, not a side effect of the stuffing.

Press the side button to cycle through four modes: Responsive Hug, Heartbeat, Deep Calm and Box Breathing. Each one serves a different need, so choose whichever feels right in the moment.

Gentle vibration moves through his belly, and each mode has its own rhythm. Responsive Hug answers how you hold him. Heartbeat slows over about five minutes. Deep Calm builds firmly, then eases for much longer. Box Breathing keeps a steady four-count by touch. See each one on the Meet Norman page.
