Meet Norman

Soft to hold.
Steady to follow.

Norman is a big, soft, chunky Highland cow. Hold him against your chest and he already feels soothing, before you press a single button. Then add soothing haptic vibration when you want a little more. Be ready to share him: everyone who meets him wants a turn.

What's inside

Soft on the outside. Considered within.

Weight

Deep pressure comfort

Microscopic glass beads in six compartments give him a settled, even weight. He sits in your lap or against your chest and stays put.

Touch

Answers when you squeeze

A pressure sensor under the heart on his upper back notices how firmly you hold, so Responsive Hug can match you.

Quiet

Nothing to manage

No app, no screen and no wireless. WiFi and Bluetooth are off in firmware. One button is all there is to learn.

Size and build

Made to be held.

Long enough to rest across your lap, and about the weight of a bag of flour. Enough weight to feel.
