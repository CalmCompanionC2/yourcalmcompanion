Our story

It started with one question.

In Suz's own words.

I have always needed a little more than the world typically offers. More weight, more movement, more time.

One night at a ballet I had a weighted Highland cow in my lap and a haptic wristband on my ankle. The gentle vibration was helping, and then one question came to me. What if the cow I was holding could do that too?

I went home and built a first prototype with a pressure sensor, a small motor and an Arduino. I pressed it and felt it pulse back, gently, in answer to me. Something clicked.

I couldn't find anything like it. So I kept building to see if the cow in my lap could really do that.

It could. And it was mine to build.

The founder

About Suz.

Suz Tocco is a principal product designer, a maker, and someone who is wired differently. She built Norman because she needed him, and because nothing like him existed.

It took many prototypes to find the right blend of motors and gentle vibration. She designed and reworked haptic pattern after haptic pattern until each mode felt just right, then made it as simple as possible to use. You hold him and he responds.

“Some of us need more. More weight. More warmth. More time. Norman was built for more.”
