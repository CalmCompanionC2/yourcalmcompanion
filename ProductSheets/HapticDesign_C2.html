03
Deep Calm
auto-starts · 10 min · fades at 9 min · LOCKED FINAL — PMR · LED: purple
Built from calming-haptics research rather than a spatial-wave metaphor — after six engines and several weeks chasing a traveling-wave concept that never quite landed, this steps back to the simplest validated mechanism available: Progressive Muscle Relaxation's asymmetric tension-then-longer-release shape, translated into a haptic sequence — a quick rise, a brief hold, then a release nearly twice as long as the build. ERM carries the entire primary sensation, with a rare, soft LRA touch as reinforcement, not a second rhythm.

Grounded in decades of clinical relaxation research since Jacobson's original 1930s work — brief tension followed by a markedly longer release produces greater relaxation than release alone, replicated repeatedly in anxiety, insomnia, and stress literature. Five other engines were built and tested first (fixed 0.1Hz rate, moving pressure band, tide+ripple, ERM-only continuous presence, sparse irregular touches, and a symmetric decelerating swell) — all removed once this one was confirmed as the strongest candidate.
A quick build, a brief hold, then a slow release nearly twice as long — like a held breath finally let go.

04
Box Breathing
FSR hold to run · resumes within 2s, restarts after · LOCKED FINAL · LED: blue
ERM ramps with the breath itself — rising on inhale, falling on exhale. LRA is the opposite: completely silent during inhale/exhale so nothing competes with the breathing rhythm, then a literal one-click-per-second metronome on all three motors together during both holds — the exact reverse of an earlier draft.
Releasing the FSR pauses the sequence. Re-engaging within about 2 seconds resumes from the preserved phase position; a longer pause instead restarts fresh at the next inhale, giving a returning user a clear, predictable starting point rather than needing to guess where they left off.

Design Principles
01
Single motor rarely reads as felt
Confirmed repeatedly on real hardware: a single motor firing alone — even at high intensity — often doesn't register. Multi-motor, simultaneous firing is what consistently reads as felt and distinct.
02
Built for felt precision, not just presence
Each motor has a distinct role — anchor, accent, ambient — not just a position. The vertical spacing and dual motor types exist to let each mode choose exactly how a sensation should feel, whether that's today's center-anchored reinforcement or a directional traveling wave in a mode not yet built.
03
Silence is part of the design
Gaps between patterns are intentional therapeutic pauses. The absence of vibration is as meaningful as the vibration itself.
04
ERM + LRA layers
ERMs provide consistent ambient presence through stuffing. LRAs add directionality and texture on top. Together they create sensations neither motor type achieves alone.
05
Overlap is intentional
DRV2605L chips play effects autonomously after the mux switches away — true simultaneous motor overlap is achievable in triggered mode without holding the channel.
06
Feel IS the product
The haptic sensation is the therapeutic intervention. Every tuning decision — timing, intensity, effect choice — directly affects nervous system response.
